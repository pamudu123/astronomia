# Reading a chart: meanings, tables and examples

Background for the site's assistant. Everything here describes **tradition and belief**, not proven fact. Practitioners differ on details; this is the common core of Vedic (Jyotish) practice as taught in Sri Lanka and India. The chart's facts always come from the person's result, never from this file. This file only says what those facts traditionally mean.

The site uses the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri), whole-sign houses and the mean node. In whole-sign houses, house 1 is the whole rising sign, house 2 is the next sign, and so on.

---

## 1. How astrologers actually read a chart

Practitioners and teaching sites describe the same order of work:

1. **Rising sign (lagna) first**: the person's outer self and approach to life. It also sets every house.
2. **Moon sign and birth star**: the mind, feelings and needs. In Sri Lanka "your rashi" means the Moon sign.
3. **The house for the question** (7th for marriage, 10th for work) with its **sign, its ruling planet (house lord), the planets sitting in it** and the natural significator of the topic.
4. **How strong the planet is**: own sign, exalted, debilitated, retrograde.
5. **Timing**: the running life period (dasha) and sub-period, then the slow planets' current position (transits, called gochara).
6. **Judge the whole chart**, not one placement. Newspaper astrologers in Sri Lanka warn against reading a single feature in isolation (the Mars check is the classic case).

Many astrologers also read the ninth-division chart (navamsa) for marriage and inner strength. The site does not show it. It uses it only for `vargottama` and for one Mars check rule (Mars strong in the ninth-division chart), so mention it only when one of those facts does.

How the readings are delivered matters as much as the method. Practitioner guidance (consultation ethics articles, readings advertised by working astrologers) agrees on a few things: speak in plain language and explain the terms, treat chart indications as tendencies and timing windows, keep the focus on the questions the person came with, and end with something practical. Sri Lankan newspaper columns tend to state rules as settled and categorical. **Do not copy that tone.** The assistant should be more careful than the columns.

## 3. The nine planets

Natural benefics (usually supportive): Jupiter, Venus, a well-placed Mercury and Moon. Natural malefics (usually demanding): Saturn, Mars, Rahu, Ketu, and the Sun in a mild form. "Malefic" means a planet that asks for effort and brings lessons, not one that brings misfortune. Never use the word "evil".

| Planet | Sinhala | Stands for | At its best | When strained |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | Ravi | Self, confidence, authority, father, leadership, recognition | Dignified, a natural leader | Ego, pride, clashes with authority |
| Moon | Chandra | Mind, feelings, mother, nurturing, public, comfort | Emotionally intelligent, caring | Moody, anxious, over-sensitive |
| Mars | Kuja | Energy, courage, drive, competition, siblings, property | Brave, hard-working, decisive | Impatient, argumentative, impulsive |
| Mercury | Budha | Intellect, speech, learning, trade, humour, skills | Quick, articulate, good with numbers and words | Overthinking, nervous, scattered |
| Jupiter | Guru | Wisdom, teachers, luck, growth, children, faith, finance | Generous, wise, fortunate | Over-optimism, over-promising, excess |
| Venus | Sikuru | Love, partnership, beauty, comfort, arts, spouse, vehicles | Charming, affectionate, creative | Indulgence, dependence, relationship strain |
| Saturn | Senasuru, Shani | Discipline, work, time, responsibility, the long haul, delay | Reliable, patient, builds lasting things | Heavy, worried, delayed, self-critical |
| Rahu | Rahu | Ambition, the unconventional, the foreign, sudden rises, hunger for more | Ambitious, innovative, makes bold leaps | Restless, obsessive, never satisfied |
| Ketu | Ketu | Detachment, spirituality, past skills, letting go, research | Insightful, self-sufficient, wise | Withdrawn, confused about direction |

Rahu and Ketu are the two points where the Moon's path crosses the Sun's. They are not physical bodies and are always exactly opposite each other.

### Strength of a planet in its sign

Use only when the planet's sign is in the chart. A planet in its **own sign** or **exalted** sign tends to give its themes smoothly and strongly. A **debilitated** planet gives them with more struggle or later in life. A planet that is debilitated is often described by practitioners as being "helped" by other conditions that cancel it (many exist), so never call it simply "weak".

| Planet | Own sign | Exalted | Debilitated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | Simha | Mesha | Tula |
| Moon | Kataka | Vrishabha | Vrischika |
| Mars | Mesha, Vrischika | Makara | Kataka |
| Mercury | Mithuna, Kanya | Kanya | Meena |
| Jupiter | Dhanu, Meena | Kataka | Makara |
| Venus | Vrishabha, Tula | Meena | Kanya |
| Saturn | Makara, Kumbha | Tula | Mesha |

**Retrograde (vakra).** A planet that appears to move backwards from Earth. Traditionally its results come in a more inward, delayed or repeated way, often later in life. It is not "bad". Describe it as "works more slowly and from the inside".

## 4. The twelve houses: life areas

Houses stay fixed and signs rotate. House 1 is the rising sign. Use the life area, not the number.

| House | Name | Life areas, in everyday words |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Thanu | The self: body, looks, personality, how you start things |
| 2 | Dhana | Family you were born into, savings, speech, food, early values |
| 3 | Sahaja | Siblings, courage, effort, skills, short trips, communication |
| 4 | Sukha | Home, mother, property, vehicles, inner peace, early education |
| 5 | Putra | Children, creativity, intelligence, studies, romance, past merit |
| 6 | Shathru | Daily work and service, health habits, debts, competition, rivals |
| 7 | Kalathra | Marriage, life partner, close partnerships, business partners |
| 8 | Mrithyu | Transformation, sudden change, shared money, research, hidden matters |
| 9 | Dharma | Luck, father, teachers, higher learning, beliefs, long journeys |
| 10 | Karma | Career, status, reputation, what you are known for |
| 11 | Labha | Income and gains, friends, networks, fulfilment of wishes, elder siblings |
| 12 | Vyaya | Spending, rest, sleep, living abroad, solitude, endings, spiritual release |

Groups: **angular** houses (1, 4, 7, 10) are strong and visible. **Trine** houses (1, 5, 9) are the luckiest. **Challenging** houses (6, 8, 12) are where life asks more of you. A planet in a challenging house is not a problem; it often shows where effort turns into skill.
Careful wording for house 8: say "change, transformation, and things that happen out of your control". Never say death, dying or lifespan, even though the tradition links this house to them (see section 13). For house 12: say "spending, rest, time away from home, solitude". For house 6: say "daily work, routines and competition".

## 6. Planets in houses: how to combine

Take the planet's theme (section 3) and the house's life area (section 4). Fit them in one plain sentence, then add the flavour of the sign.

| Pattern | Reading |
|---|---|
| Sun in the 10th | Draws attention at work; leadership and recognition themes; may dislike being told what to do |
| Moon in the 4th | Needs a peaceful home; strong bond with mother and home; settled by place |
| Mars in the 3rd | Courageous and hard-working; competitive with siblings or peers; good at sport or effort-based skills |
| Mercury in the 2nd | Speech is a strength; earns through talk, teaching, trade, numbers |
| Jupiter in the 5th | Teacher's mind; supportive toward children and students; luck in studies and creativity |
| Venus in the 7th | Values harmony and affection in partnership; attractive to others; relationships are central to life |
| Saturn in the 10th | Slow, steady rise; hard-working; greater responsibility, recognition arrives later |
| Saturn in the 7th | Takes partnership seriously; marriage can come later or with a more mature partner; relationships ask patience |
| Rahu in the 12th | Pull toward foreign places, unusual spiritual interests, or an active inner life |
| Ketu in the 4th | Detached from material comfort; a restless or unusual relationship with home |

## 7. The Mars check (Kuja dosha) in a personal reading

The site shows whether Mars is in one of the traditional houses (1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 12) and whether conditions reduce it. In a personal reading:

- Describe it as "Mars adds strong energy to the partnership area; tradition asks for care that both partners handle the heat in arguments and make space for the other's pace".
- If `present` is false: say nothing alarming; don't bring it up unless asked.
- If `reduced` is true: say conditions soften it.
- Never say it will harm a marriage or the partner. Sri Lankan newspaper astrologers themselves say hasty readings of this have caused needless worry and blocked marriages. Remind that the whole chart matters and that charts of both partners are compared.
- The cancelling conditions the site checks (rule set `kuja_v2`): the rising sign is Kataka or Simha; Mars in its own or exalted sign (Mesha, Vrischika, Makara); Jupiter with Mars or looking at it; Mars in a sign excused for its house; Mars with the Moon; Mars in Simha or Kumbha; Jupiter or Venus in the lagna. The last three follow B. V. Raman (Muhurtha). Raman counts the dosha from the 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th and 12th only (the site also counts the 1st), weakest from the lagna and strongest from Venus. Sri Lankan writers list many more (one site lists twenty), including both partners having it, the lagna lord strong in an angle or trine, and the belief that it weakens after about age 28. Those are not checked: mention them only as what some astrologers say.
- Sri Lankan writers commonly suggest a bodhi puja on Tuesdays and patience with the partner. Describe it as custom if asked, never as needed.

## 8. Life periods (dasha): what each planet's period emphasises

The Vimshottari life period is the main timing clock. Each of nine planets rules a stretch of years in a fixed order (Ketu 7, Venus 20, Sun 6, Moon 10, Mars 7, Rahu 18, Jupiter 16, Saturn 19, Mercury 17). Sub-periods inside each main period run in the same order.

**Read a period in four steps:**

1. **The planet's natural themes** (table below).
2. **The house the planet sits in** in this person's chart: that is the life area where the period's energy shows up.
3. **The houses it rules** (the lord table): the other life areas pulled in. Use the one or two most relevant to the question.
4. **Its strength** (own, exalted, debilitated, retrograde), then the **sub-period planet** for the current colour.

| Period | Traditionally emphasises | A stretch people describe as |
|---|---|---|
| Ketu (7 yrs) | Detachment, spiritual curiosity, simplifying, letting go of what no longer fits | Inward; sorting out what matters; sometimes unsettled |
| Venus (20 yrs) | Relationships, comfort, creativity, beauty, vehicles, home | Pleasant; focus on partnership and enjoying life; can encourage indulgence |
| Sun (6 yrs) | Identity, recognition, authority, father, government or leadership | Stepping into one's own authority; needing respect |
| Moon (10 yrs) | Feelings, family, mother, home, the public, nurturing | Emotional; focus on family and inner security |
| Mars (7 yrs) | Drive, ambition, competition, property, technical or physical work | Energetic and pushy; things move fast; risk of impatience |
| Rahu (18 yrs) | Ambition, change, foreign links, unconventional paths, big appetites | Restless, ambitious, many changes; success through bold, unusual routes |
| Jupiter (16 yrs) | Learning, teachers, growth, advice, children, good fortune | Expansion, teaching and being taught, generosity |
| Saturn (19 yrs) | Responsibility, structure, hard work, delayed rewards, maturity | Slow, demanding, character-building; solid results later |
| Mercury (17 yrs) | Study, communication, trade, skills, curiosity, young ideas | Busy mind; learning, business, networks, travel for work |

A period is never simply good or bad; it is read in the chart. A "Saturn period" can be the time someone builds a career. A "Venus period" can bring a heavy workload if Venus sits in the 6th.

**Look ahead:** the next period's change of tone is often the most helpful single thing to say.

### Reading a sub-period

The main period sets the season; the sub-period is the weather inside it. Read the sub-period planet the same four ways (themes, the house it sits in, the houses it rules, its strength), then blend it with the main period. A supportive sub-period inside a demanding main period gives relief; a demanding one inside a pleasant period asks for care.

| Sub-period planet | What tends to come forward |
|---|---|
| Ketu | Letting go, inner questions, spiritual interest, less interest in worldly results; a time to simplify |
| Venus | Relationships and marriage prospects, comfort, vehicles, art and enjoyment, money through partnerships |
| Sun | Authority, recognition, dealings with officials or seniors, matters around the father, confidence |
| Moon | Peace of mind, home and family, mother, travel, emotions closer to the surface |
| Mars | Courage and drive, property and land, competition, quick action; keep patience in disagreements |
| Rahu | Many changes, sudden opportunities, travel and foreign links, ambition; check plans carefully |
| Jupiter | Learning, good advice, success in studies, respect, family blessings, good decisions |
| Saturn | Steady effort, responsibility, delays that later bring stability, long-term planning |
| Mercury | Education, trade, writing and speaking, clever decisions, business and networks |

Example of a timeline line: "From 26 October 2025 to 1 February 2028, the Jupiter sub-period: a good stretch for study and for taking good advice. Jupiter rules your 9th house of luck and teachers, so the help of a mentor may matter a lot." Dates come only from the chart's `life_period_timeline`.

## 9. Planet changes and the Moon sign (gochara, erashtaka)

For dates and for the rising-sign readings, always use the planet change tools. The notes below are background.

- Slow planets colour a year or two at a time. Saturn spends about 2.5 years in each sign, Jupiter about a year, Rahu and Ketu about 18 months.
- They are read by the house counted **from the Moon sign** (and often also from the rising sign). Traditionally Jupiter passing the 2nd, 5th, 7th, 9th and 11th house from the Moon is seen as supportive, and Saturn in the 3rd, 6th and 11th as supportive. Saturn in the 12th, 1st and 2nd from the Moon is the **erashtaka** (about 7.5 years); in the 8th is **ashtama shani**. These are rules of thumb that practitioners combine with the rest of the chart.
- **How to say it:** "Saturn's seven-and-a-half-year stretch asks for patience, steady habits and realistic planning. Many people feel the pressure early and the benefit later. Plenty of people do very well in it."
- **How not to say it:** never "dreaded", "bad", "suffering", "danger".

## 12. Words that keep a reading honest

Use these forms. They keep predictions as possibilities:

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| "You will get married in 2028" | "This period is traditionally one where relationship themes become more active." |
| "You will be wealthy" | "The chart shows steady earning potential when you keep working at it." |
| "This is a bad period" | "This is a slower period that asks for patience. Knowing it ahead gives you time to prepare, and people often use it to build foundations." |
| "Your marriage will have trouble" | "I'm telling you now so you can prepare: keep talking early and don't let small things pile up. That often keeps this kind of pressure from growing." |
| "Watch out, danger" | "Here is something worth keeping an eye on, and here is what you can do about it." |
| "Saturn will hurt you" | "Saturn brings pressure that rewards steady effort." |
| "You must..." | "Many people in this period find it helps to..." |
| "You are lazy / stubborn / jealous" | "You may notice a tendency to... and it helps when you..." |

Short honesty line for forward-looking answers (use occasionally, not every time): "This is how the tradition reads your chart. Treat it as a way to reflect, not a guarantee. Your choices matter more than any chart."

## 13. Hard subjects: health, relationship strain, money strain, care periods

People ask about these, so Astro answers. Use only placements you can read from the chart (each planet's own `house`, each house's `lord`, `lord_in_house`, `planets` and `aspected_by`, the running period). A pointer counts only if the chart's own entry says it: check the planet's `house` before naming where it sits. A pointer is never proof. One pointer is a small thing; several together in one area, or one that lines up with a running period, deserves a mention. If nothing points that way, say the chart shows no strong pointer there.

Always: possibility language, what helps, a human astrologer for the whole chart, and a doctor, lawyer or financial adviser for the real matter.

**Spirit of the warning.** An astrologer who mentions a difficulty does it so the person can prepare and steer around it. Say so: "I'm telling you this so you can be ready". A tendency can often be softened by awareness and good choices, so each pointer below comes with things the person can do. Finish every hard answer on hope and on their own agency. Don't overpromise: "can often be softened", not "will be avoided".

### Health: areas the tradition links to each planet

These show where to look after oneself, not what illness someone has. Never name a disease as a prediction.

| Planet | Areas the tradition links to it |
|---|---|
| Sun | Vitality, heart, eyes, bones, energy levels |
| Moon | Mind and mood, sleep, fluids, digestion, emotional wellbeing |
| Mars | Blood, heat and inflammation, injuries, accidents, impatience and haste |
| Mercury | Nerves, skin, breathing, stress, overthinking |
| Jupiter | Digestion, liver, weight, excess and over-indulgence |
| Venus | Kidneys, reproductive health, skin, comfort and over-indulgence |
| Saturn | Joints, bones, teeth, slow long-running conditions, fatigue |
| Rahu | Hard-to-pin-down troubles, anxiety, allergies, restlessness |
| Ketu | Low energy, troubles that are hard to explain, withdrawal |

**Pointers worth mentioning:** Saturn, Mars, Rahu or Ketu in the 1st or 6th house; the lord of the 1st or 6th house placed in the 6th, 8th or 12th; the Moon with Saturn, Mars or Rahu (mind and mood); a running period of the 6th, 8th or 12th house lord, or of a planet sitting in those houses; erashtaka or ashtama shani running.

**How to say it:** "Your chart draws some attention to [area], especially during [period]. I'm telling you now so you can look after it early, and that is a real advantage: regular routines, enough rest, a check-up now and then, and not ignoring small signs often keep things from growing. If anything is worrying you, please see a doctor. A human astrologer can read the whole chart with you."

### Relationship strain or separation

**Pointers:** Saturn, Mars, Rahu or Ketu in the 7th house; the 7th lord in the 6th, 8th or 12th house; Venus in Kanya (debilitated) or sitting with Saturn, Mars or Rahu; the Mars check (as described in section 7); a running period of the 6th, 8th or 12th lord when the partnership matters are in the question.

**How to say it:** "The chart shows some pressure in the partnership area, so differences or distance could come up in [period]. Knowing this early is a strength: couples who talk openly, avoid letting small things build up, give each other space, and lean on family support often get through such periods closer than before. A human astrologer can read both charts together." If the person is already married or asks about divorce: don't say it will fail. Say what the chart shows as a possibility, and that the decision is theirs and their family's.

### Money strain

**Pointers:** the 2nd or 11th lord in the 6th, 8th or 12th; Saturn, Mars, Rahu or Ketu in the 2nd; Jupiter in Makara or Venus in Kanya; Rahu or Mars in the 12th; a running period of the 12th lord (spending) or the 6th lord (debts and competition).

**How to say it:** "The chart suggests [period] favours caution with money: steady savings, fewer big risks, keeping some reserve. Because you know this ahead, you can use the time to build a safety margin, pay down debts and avoid big risks, and people who do this often pass through such a period without any real trouble." Never name a stock, scheme or lawsuit, and never give tax, legal or investment advice.

### Periods that call for extra care

The tradition treats some stretches as sensitive: the period of the 8th lord or of planets sitting in the 8th house, Saturn or Rahu or Mars in the 1st or 8th, ashtama shani. **Death, dying and lifespan are never named, even when these are present.** Say only: "your chart shows a stretch that calls for extra care with health and safety. It is a sensitive area that is best talked through with a human astrologer who can read the whole chart, along with regular medical advice. Things that usually help are steady routines, rest, careful driving and travel, and not taking unnecessary risks." Then move to what is in their control.

### Remedies the tradition mentions

When asked what can be done, you may describe the customs below, once, as personal belief. Their own astrologer advises on what suits them. Never required, never guaranteed, never a product to buy.

- Almsgiving (dana) and offering food to monks, or to those in need
- Pirith chanting at home or at a temple
- A Bo-tree puja with coconut-oil lamps, as described for erashtaka in the birth-chart notes
- Observing sil on poya days, quiet reflection, kindness to parents and elders
- Steady habits: routine, rest, patience, careful spending

Gems, charms, paid pujas and similar: say these are a matter for their own astrologer, that nothing needs buying to be safe, and that anyone using fear to sell remedies is not following the spirit of the tradition.

## 14. Aspects, extra strength and special combinations

These come ready-made in the chart facts (`aspected_by`, `vargottama`, `combinations`). This section says what they mean.

### Aspects (a planet "looking at" a house)

Every planet looks at the 7th house from where it sits. Mars also looks at the 4th and 8th from itself, Jupiter at the 5th and 9th, Saturn at the 3rd and 10th. The site gives no aspects for Rahu and Ketu, where traditions differ.

- Jupiter's look is the classic blessing: it protects and grows the area it looks at.
- Venus and a strong Moon or Mercury bring ease, charm or intelligence to it.
- Saturn's look brings seriousness, delay and endurance: results come later but last.
- Mars's look brings energy and drive, sometimes heat or haste.

How to say it: "Jupiter looks at your partnership area from the 4th house, which astrologers read as protection for marriage and family harmony."

### Vargottama

A planet in the same sign in the main chart and the ninth-division chart. Tradition counts it as extra strength and consistency: the planet "keeps its word". Mention it as a quiet strength: "Your Ketu is vargottama, so its themes of insight and independence are especially steady in your life."

### Two more planet facts

- **`yoga_karaka`**: the planet rules both an angle (4, 7 or 10) and a trine (5 or 9) for this rising sign: Saturn for Vrishabha and Tula, Mars for Kataka and Simha, Venus for Makara and Kumbha. Tradition treats it as the chart's most helpful planet; its life period and sub-periods are watched for progress. "Saturn is the yoga karaka for your rising sign, the planet tradition counts as your chart's best friend, so its periods tend to bring solid progress."
- **`combust`**: the planet is within a few degrees of the Sun (Moon 12, Mars 17, Mercury 14 or 12 when retrograde, Jupiter 11, Venus 10 or 8 when retrograde, Saturn 15). Tradition says its light is hidden by the Sun: its themes show less openly, or work through the person's own will and ego. Mercury is close to the Sun very often and tradition gives a combust Mercury little weight (it is also how Budhaditya yoga forms). Say "works more quietly", never "destroyed" or "weak".

## Main caveats

1. Traditions differ on many details (which planets are significators, how to count the Mars check, which transits count). Prefer the common core given here, and say "astrologers differ" when it matters.
2. The sources behind this file are practitioner and newspaper columns, not independent evidence that astrology predicts events. Most of them are written with more certainty than is wise.
3. No chart reading replaces a human astrologer, a doctor, a lawyer or a financial adviser.
4. The nakshatra keywords and the planet-in-house patterns are a teaching summary, not a quotation from a classical text. An astrologer should review them before launch.
5. lankaastrology.com, the source of the November 2026 additions, writes with great certainty ("100% cancelled", "will bring wealth") and some of its pages use frightening wording about timing and health. Only the traditional content was taken, reworded. Never copy that tone.
