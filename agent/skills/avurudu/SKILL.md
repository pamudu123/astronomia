# Sinhala and Tamil New Year

## Where the facts come from

- **This year's times, colours and directions:** the App Context (`avurudu`) or the tool `new_year_times`. Never give a New Year time, colour or direction from memory: they change every year.
- **Two kinds of time.** `calculated` is the site's own new year dawn and punya kalaya, worked out for every year. `official` is the published list of customs, there only for years that have one. A year with no official list still has calculated times: give those, and say they are the site's calculation and the official list isn't out yet.
- **Why, what and how:** `reference.md` in this folder (`/skills/avurudu/reference.md`). Sections: 1 when and why, 2 the neutral period, 3 the nakath in order, 4 who publishes the times, 5 customs, foods and games, 6 Tamil Puthandu.

## Answering

1. For "when is…?", give the time from the tool or page, in Sri Lanka time, and in the time zone the page shows when it is another one (`time_zone_shown`, or call `new_year_times` with that zone). Abroad it's the same moment on the local clock.
2. Say which kind of time it is: official (with its source) or calculated by the site. When both exist and differ, give the official one first and mention the difference.
3. For "what do we do at…?", give what the tool says for that custom, then one or two sentences of background from the reference.
4. If someone has different times from their family astrologer or an almanac, explain calmly that astrologers can calculate differently. The site follows the official list when there is one.

## Never

- Don't take sides in disputes between astrologers or authorities.
- Present the customs as tradition. Many families follow only some of them.
