# The match factors in everyday words

Background for explaining a porondam result like a family astrologer would: what each factor is about, what agreement or disagreement tends to look like in a marriage, and what couples do about it. The **result** (agrees or not, and the reason) always comes from the page. This file only gives the meaning. Everything here describes tradition, not proven fact.

Factor names below are the ones the site shows. Where the traditional meaning touches long life or health, use the softer wording given, never words about death or illness.

## The four themes

Grouping factors by theme makes a result easier to follow than a list of twenty names. Use these groups when explaining a whole result.

| Theme | Factors |
|---|---|
| Getting along day to day (temperament, outlook, how minds meet) | Gana, Varna, Rashi, Rashyadhipathi, Graha, Bhutha, Pakshi |
| Attraction and affection | Yoni, Vashya, Linga |
| Family and children | Mahendra, Vruksha, Gothra, Nadi |
| Wellbeing and a lasting bond | Nakath, Stree Deergha, Ayusha, Dina, and the two essential checks, Rajju and Vedha |

A useful way to speak: "Your strongest area is attraction and affection, where all three factors agree. The area to give attention to is day-to-day temperament, where two factors don't agree."

## Each person's emotional style

When both Moon signs are on the page, you can add each person's emotional style from the Moon sign (see the sign table in the `personal-reading` reference, section 2), then say how the two meet. Keep it light and balanced. For example: "With a Kataka Moon you feel things deeply and need a calm home. Her Mesha Moon reacts fast and moves on. That can work well: she brings energy, you bring steadiness, as long as she gives you time to talk it through."

Elements make this easy: fire (Mesha, Simha, Dhanu), earth (Vrishabha, Kanya, Makara), air (Mithuna, Tula, Kumbha), water (Kataka, Vrischika, Meena). Same element: easy understanding. Fire with air, or earth with water: natural support. Fire with water, or earth with air: different energies that need patience.
