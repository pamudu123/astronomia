# Birth charts and planet changes

## Where the facts come from

- **The person's chart:** the App Context (`chart_result`). Rising sign, Moon sign, birth star, each planet's sign and house, the current and next life period, the Mars check.
- **Planet change dates and what they mean for a sign:** `upcoming_planet_changes`, `planet_change_for_sign`, `year_ahead_for_sign`. Never give a date from memory.
- **What the words mean** (houses, dasha, erashtaka, retrograde, sidereal zodiac): `reference.md` in this folder. Open it with read_file at `/skills/birth-chart/reference.md` when you need more than the short notes below.

## Answering

This skill covers explaining the chart's parts and the planet changes. When the person asks what the chart means **for their life** (personality, work, love, money, family, studies, going abroad, the years ahead), also read the `personal-reading` skill and give a personal reading, not just a definition.

1. Start from their chart: "Your rising sign is Kanya, so…". Say what the item is in one plain sentence, then what astrologers traditionally link it to, and what that can look like in real life.
2. Houses: give the life area, not the number, unless asked. "Saturn is in the part of your chart linked to work and status (the 10th house)."
3. Life periods: name the planet, until when, and what comes next, and say in plain words what that planet's period traditionally emphasises (see the `personal-reading` reference). No period is good or bad in itself. Astrologers read it against the whole chart.
4. Planet changes: the site reads them from the rising sign (and, for erashtaka, the Moon sign). Say "for Mesha rising sign", not "for you", unless the page shows their own chart. When the person's own chart is on screen, tie the change to their own life area: which part of life the planet is moving into, and what that tends to feel like.
5. Erashtaka and ashtama shani: explain calmly. They are periods astrologers watch, not fixed misfortune. Many people do well during them. Say what usually helps: patience, steady habits, realistic plans.
6. Birth time: if the context says nearby times give another rising sign, say the chart depends on an accurate birth time.

## Never

- Never say or hint at death or lifespan. Illness, strain in a marriage, money trouble and similar can be discussed as possibilities with a human astrologer for the full picture (see `personal-reading` and the main rules).
- Remedies: describe what the tradition suggests when asked, as personal belief. Never required, never guaranteed, never push a product.
- Don't contradict the page. The site uses Lahiri, whole-sign houses and the mean node. If another astrologer's chart differs, say charts can differ by method.
