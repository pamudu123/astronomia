# Delivery, refunds and cancellation

Public draft C from the legal review of 20 November 2026. For use only once purchases open, which waits for a payment provider that approves astrology reports in writing (`docs/15-payments.md`, section 0). The second half is the text for the checkout and the forms.

---

For use once purchases open · Effective [date]

Sold by [legal trader, address, email and telephone]. We show the full price, currency, included features, delivery time and access period before payment. Your order confirmation explains how to access your purchase. Contact [support email] with your order reference if it does not arrive. Please do not send card numbers or your full birth chart.

## One-off reports

Check the birth date, time zone, birthplace and report language in the order preview. Where available, you may ask for immediate delivery. If local law gives you a withdrawal period, we will tell you how it applies and obtain any separate consent and acknowledgement needed before supplying the digital content early. After valid loss of a change-of-mind right, a correctly supplied report generally cannot be returned just because you changed your mind or entered the wrong details. Contact us promptly about input mistakes so we can see whether a correction is possible. Rights concerning a defective or misdescribed report continue.

## Problems and duplicate payments

If we charged you twice for one order, we will refund the duplicate. If we failed to deliver, delivered the wrong report or materially failed to supply what was advertised, contact us. We will provide the correction, replacement, price reduction or refund to which you are entitled. If we cannot supply the purchase, we will refund it. A difference in traditional interpretation alone does not establish a defect in a correctly described report. Report suspected unauthorised payments to us or your payment provider promptly. You may also use your bank's dispute process.

## Subscriptions, if sold

The order screen states when renewal occurs and what you will pay. Cancel through [working cancellation link] or contact [support email]. We confirm cancellation and stop future renewals when cancellation takes effect. Ordinary cancellation leaves access until the end of the paid period. Statutory withdrawal, faulty service and closure are handled separately. Starting an ongoing service does not by itself forfeit all withdrawal rights. If you expressly requested an early start, local law may permit a proportionate charge for service already supplied. If we stop supplying a prepaid subscription, we return the unused portion.

## Withdrawing and receiving a refund

Where a statutory withdrawal right applies, use [working withdrawal link], or send a clear statement to [email/address], giving your name, order reference, order date and the item you wish to cancel. Where the ordinary period is 14 days, it generally starts on contract conclusion for these digital purchases. We confirm receipt. We issue refunds without undue delay and within any legal deadline. For an eligible UK/EU statutory withdrawal, this is generally within 14 days of notice. We use the original payment method unless an alternative is agreed without a fee to you. Bank posting time can vary. Your legal remedies do not expire after seven days.

# Checkout and form text

For the build. Each piece goes where it is named. Ticks always start unticked.

| Where | Text and rule |
|---|---|
| Order button | "Pay LKR [total] for this report." For subscriptions: "[amount] every [period], renews on [date] until cancelled." Show delivery, taxes, access period, cancellation and seller contacts next to the button |
| Terms tick | "I agree to the Terms of Use and the Delivery, Refunds and Cancellation Policy." Link the Privacy Notice separately |

I understand that I lose my statutory right to change my mind once supply begins." If unticked, offer delayed delivery or explain the options.
