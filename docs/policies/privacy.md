## Getting a result

You can use our free calculators without an account. We use the birth date, time and town or coordinates you enter. A name is optional. For a match, we use the information supplied for both people or their birth stars. We send these details in the calculation request and return the result to your browser. An ordinary calculation does not create a saved report. A PDF is generated when requested and returned to you. Copies you download stay under your control.

## Why we use information

For your own chart we use the information needed to deliver the calculation, PDF or saved report you request. Where this forms part of our agreement with you, we rely on performance of that agreement. For optional sharing and for another person's identifying information, we ask for permission for the stated purpose. For children's information we rely on a parent's or legal guardian's consent. We use limited operational information for our legitimate interests in preventing abuse, securing the service and answering support requests. Where the law requires us to keep records or respond to lawful demands, we use information for that purpose. You may omit the optional fields. Without the birth inputs a particular calculation requires, we cannot produce that result. We do not sell personal data or use advertising trackers.

## Information entered by someone else

An adult family member may supply your details for a calculation. They must have your permission and show you this notice. Permission to calculate a chart is separate from permission to save it, use AI or share it. If someone used your details without permission, contact us. If a link exposes them, you can ask us to restrict access while we review it.

## Children

Sidera is for adults aged 18 or over. A parent or legal guardian may use our child tools to request a calculation. We ask for the guardian's consent first. A child's name is unnecessary for naming letters. Our child tools do not send the result to AI or offer a public share link. You may withdraw guardian consent by contacting us. Withdrawal applies going forward.

## Your browser and saved reports

Your browser tab keeps the latest result and visit-related AI text. If you choose Remember my chart, the browser keeps that chart until you use Forget or clear the site's storage. The browser also holds the deletion code for links you create. Other people with access to your device may see retained results. Choosing Save creates a guest record and sets a private browser-key cookie and a marker for My reports. We store a hash of the key. The guest record has no sign-in email or password. Saved reports can nevertheless contain names and identifying birth details. Up to 100 reports can be saved. Guest cookies renew while used, and inactive guest accounts and reports are deleted after 12 months. [Insert verified cookie lifetimes.]

## Sharing

You see a preview before creating a link. Anyone who obtains it can read or forward the displayed information. Even with a name hidden, a birth date, time and town may identify someone. Links expire after 30 days. The creating browser can revoke a link sooner. Deleting a saved report and revoking a share link are separate actions. Revocation cannot recall screenshots or downloads someone already made.

## AI helper and readings

When you request an AI explanation or send a message, our AI server sends the relevant calculated chart facts and any message you write to OpenAI. Sidera does not automatically attach names, birth dates, birth times or birthplaces. Your own message can disclose these. Chart facts can also be identifying in context. Please leave out names, children's details, contact information, and sensitive information about yourself or anyone else. Our AI server keeps conversation context in memory for [verified time limit]. Our application does not log message text. We request disabled response storage. OpenAI's standard API terms do not use customer content for model training unless the customer opts in. Separate abuse-monitoring logs can retain content for up to 30 days by default, subject to provider exceptions.
