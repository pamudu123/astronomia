## 5. What this suggests for your pricing

These are suggestions to test, not conclusions.

1. **Keep the core free.** Chart, basic porondam and daily palapala must be free to compete and to win SEO traffic.
2. **Sell what is hard to get free:**
   - Detailed PDF report in Sinhala and English
   - Unlimited AI chat after a free daily limit
   - Saved profiles and multi proposal comparison
   - Human astrologer review of an AI report
3. **Price anchors:**
   - AI or software report: well below the human range, for example LKR 500 to 1,500, to look clearly cheaper than LKR 3,000 to 10,000.
   - Human review add on: priced inside the market band (LKR 3,000 to 7,000), you keep a commission.
   - Subscription: Jyothishya's $1.49 to $8.49 per month or year pattern suggests a low priced monthly plan can work. In LKR, a few hundred rupees per month would be a guess to test.
4. **Payment methods** matter in Sri Lanka. Plan for local cards, bank transfer, and mobile wallet or carrier billing. Check which gateways are available (for example PayHere, a local gateway).
5. **Diaspora pricing.** The Kapruka listing in USD hints at overseas buyers paying in dollars. Consider separate USD pricing.
