# Competitor Research: Sri Lankan Hadahan (Jathaka) and Porondam Websites

Date of research: 2026-11-03

## Terms

| Term | Sinhala | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Hadahan / Jathaka / Kendara | හඳහන / ජාතකය / කේන්දරය | Personal birth chart (horoscope) from date, exact time and place of birth |
| Porondam | පොරොන්දම් | Marriage compatibility matching of two birth charts |
| Nakath | නැකත් | Auspicious times, or the lunar star (nakshatra) |
| Palapala | පලාපල | Predictions (daily, monthly) |
| Kuja dosha | කුජ දෝෂය | Mars affliction considered harmful to marriage |
| Lagna | ලග්නය | Ascendant |

## IMPORTANT: how reliable is this research

I could **not open any of the competitor sites directly**. The research environment blocked every site domain (DNS lookup failed for lakpura.com, mystar.lk, sinhalahoroscope.com, lankahandahana.com, porondam.lk, porondam.net, purahada.lk, iranama.lk and lankawebsites.com).

Everything below comes from **web search result summaries**. That means:

- Feature lists are what the sites say about themselves, as relayed by search summaries.
- Some details are second hand and a few search summaries contradicted each other (noted below).
- Pricing, UI, accuracy and traffic were **not** verified.
- Before you finalise scope, open each site yourself for 10 minutes and tick off the "Verify yourself" checklist at the end of this file.

Confidence tags used: **[High]** appeared in several independent results, **[Medium]** one source, **[Low]** a single vague mention.

## Competitors found

### 1. MyStar (mystar.lk)
Self described as "Sri Lanka's own Vedic astrology platform". Probably the closest thing to what you plan to build.

- Free birth chart (jathaka) **[Medium]**
- Porondam matching using **20 factors**, with Rajju and Vedha treated as pass or fail checks **[Medium]**
- Nakath finder that calculates auspicious windows for life events **[Medium]**
- Daily predictions **[Medium]**
- Sinhala and English interface **[Medium]**
- Uses the **Thirukanitham ayanamsa** (a Tamil/Lahiri style sidereal setting) **[Medium]**
- Claims most other online platforms only implement 8 or 10 matching factors, whereas the "authentic" Sri Lankan system has 20. This is marketing, but it shows 20 factor matching is the selling point.
- Says articles are AI assisted and reviewed by an editorial team.
- Source: a promotional blog post on lankawebsites.com (self reported).

### 2. Lakpura (lakpura.com)
A Sri Lankan retail site that also runs free astrology tools. Very SEO heavy.

- Porondam calculator that takes two birth charts and calculates from actual Moon and planet positions **[Medium]**
- Shows **Sinhala porondam, Tamil porutham and Kuja dosha side by side** **[Medium]**
- States that nothing entered is stored (privacy selling point) **[Medium]**
- A second mode: pick two rashis (moon signs) and read a pre written compatibility page. It has a page for every rashi pair, for example "mithuna tula porondam" **[High]**
- Horoscope and palapala (predictions) pages, plus educational pages on the jathaka and almanac **[Medium]**
- Pages in many languages (zh, fr, hi, ar, sv, it, nl, ja and more) aimed at foreign visitors and diaspora **[High]**
- Advises users to consult a family astrologer for the final decision.

### 3. sinhalahoroscope.com
Search listing says "නොමිලේ සිංහල කේන්දරය" (free Sinhala kendara). **[Low]**

- Free Sinhala birth chart, porondam matching, daily predictions (daily-palapala page exists), no registration required.
- Note: one later search could not find this site at all, so confirm it exists and is active.

### 4. Lanka Handahana (lankahandahana.com)
Listing title: "Free Online Horoscope & Porondam Galapima Sinhala". **[Low]**

- Free kendara and porondam matching in Sinhala and English.
- "Galapima" (ගැලපීම) is a common Sinhala search word for matching.

### 5. porondam.lk
Listing title: "Porondam Galapima". **[Low]**

- Describes itself as an online astrology portal with free porondam matching, horoscope checks, auspicious time finding and baby naming.
- Available as website and app (claim only, app not found in store searches).

### 6. porondam.net
Listing title: "porondam galapima sinhala horoscope matching online free 20". **[Low]**

- Free porondam matching with 20 factor wording in the page title.
- Shows how competitors target exact search phrases.

### 7. Purahada (purahada.lk/porondam.php)
Listing title: "Match your Horoscope Online for Free". **[Low]**

- Free matching that checks the traditional 20 porondam items.
- Also checks doshas such as Kuja dosha.

### 8. Iranama (iranama.lk)
Listing title: "Free Online Horoscope Report & Birth Chart". **[Low]**

- Free Sinhala horoscope report including Kuja dosha check.
- **Premium porondam matching offered as a paid option** (freemium model).

### 9. Mobile and telecom channels
- **Jyothishya App** (iOS, id1459372211): porondam matching, finding auspicious letters for a name, baby name list, Rahu Kala times. Sinhala. Free with in app purchases. **[Medium]**
- **Mobitel Astrology Services**: IVR/SMS astrology subscription by an astrologer (Indika Thotawatta). Listed pricing was a daily prepaid plan around Rs 2.88 + tax and a monthly postpaid plan around Rs 86.40 + tax. Page is undated. **[Medium]**
- **Etisalat horoscope service**: Daily FT article from about 2011. Likely discontinued. **[Low]**
- **Sinhala dina potha / calendar apps** (Android): Sri Lankan calendars with nakath and litha. Not horoscope tools but overlap on nakath. **[Medium]**
- Helakuru (Sinhala keyboard, 400k+ daily users) shows how large the Sinhala mobile audience is.

### 10. Non Sri Lankan sites that rank for Sinhala terms
- **OnlineJyotish.com** has a `/sinhala-astrology/` section with daily horoscope by rashi, nakath and ruling planets, and a free birth chart. **[Medium]**
- Big Indian sites (Prokerala, DrikPanchang, ClickAstro) serve Sri Lankans through English. They are strong on chart accuracy but know nothing about Sinhala porondam.

## Combined feature inventory seen across competitors

### Hadahan / Jathaka
- Free birth chart from date, time, place
- Sinhala language chart and report
- Kuja dosha check
- In depth report (premium on some sites)
- Daily, monthly palapala by lagna or rashi

### Porondam
- Match two birth charts using the 20 item visi porondam
- Sinhala porondam, Tamil porutham and Kuja dosha shown together
- Rashi pair quick lookup (no birth time needed)
- Pass or fail rules (Rajju, Vedha) on top of the total score
- Premium detailed matching (Iranama)
- "Nothing stored" privacy statement (Lakpura)

### Nakath and utilities
- Nakath finder for life events (marriage, new house, business start, naming, first rice, first letters)
- Baby name letter finder from birth star
- Rahu Kala and daily auspicious times
- Calendar (dina potha)

### Channels and business model
- Free web tools with ads and SEO pages
- Freemium: free chart, paid premium reports
- Mobile apps with in app purchases
- Telecom subscriptions (IVR/SMS)
- Multilingual pages for the diaspora

## Gaps and opportunities (inferred, not verified)

These are opportunities worth testing. They are based on what the search summaries did **not** mention.

1. **Accuracy transparency.** Competitors make claims ("20 factors", "authentic") but no site was reported to show its method or ayanamsa clearly. Showing the calculation per factor would build trust.
2. **Explained results.** Showing why each factor passed or failed, in plain Sinhala, rather than only a score.
3. **Pass or fail logic.** Rajju and Vedha are reported to override the total score on some sites. Make that explicit.
4. **Match against astrologer consultation.** Most tools tell users to consult a family astrologer. A booking marketplace with verified astrologers could close the loop.
5. **Saved profiles and family sharing.** Matching multiple proposals for one person is a real workflow in Sri Lankan marriage searches. Only a "nothing stored" stance was mentioned, so saved comparisons look unaddressed.
6. **Mobile first Sinhala UX and PDF/print export** of the kendara in the traditional layout (the style families bring to astrologers).
7. **Sinhala and Tamil** support, since Tamil porutham differs from Sinhala porondam.
8. **Diaspora** (UK, Australia, Middle East, Canada) searching for the same service, with timezone and historical place handling.
9. **Data sources and handling of sensitive birth data** (privacy policy, deletion on request).

## Verify yourself checklist

For each of mystar.lk, lakpura.com, sinhalahoroscope.com, lankahandahana.com, porondam.lk, porondam.net, purahada.lk, iranama.lk:

- [ ] Does the site still exist and work?
- [ ] Inputs asked for (name, date, time, place, birth star, lagna?)
- [ ] Is the result chart South Indian style, North Indian style, or Sri Lankan style?
- [ ] Which ayanamsa and calculation (compare 2 charts to a known correct chart)
- [ ] How many porondam factors are shown, and are Rajju, Vedha, Kuja dosha handled?
- [ ] Pricing for premium features
- [ ] Ad load and mobile speed
- [ ] Is there an app on Google Play?
- [ ] Is Sinhala Unicode rendering correct?
- [ ] Do they store birth data, and is there a privacy policy?
