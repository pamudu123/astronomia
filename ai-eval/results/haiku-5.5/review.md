**Ground truth.** Timetable in order with all official times: bathing 12 April; punya 09:05 to 21:53; dawn 15:29; meals 18:18; work/eating 20:18; oil 07:04 on 17 April; leaving for work 06:15 on 21 April; trees 11:38 on 22 April; new moon 9 April and 7 May.

**Rubric.** In order; Easy to find a time; What families do, then why.

**Answer** (first words 1.12s, done 4.13s)

> # The Avurudu timetable
> 
> The New Year moves at the astrological moment, so the whole festival follows its timing. Each custom has its hour, and the family does a set thing at that hour: the old year is closed, the neutral time is kept quiet, and the new year opens with the hearth, the first work and the first exchange.
> 
> The official list for 2027 has been published, and it is what we follow. Its times are in Sri Lanka time.
> 
> **🪔 The old year and the neutral time**
> 
> Bathing for the old year falls on 12 April 2027. The new year's dawn comes at 15:29 on 14 April. The neutral time, the punya kalaya, runs from 09:05 to 21:53 that day, centred on the dawn. Nothing new is begun in it: families keep quiet, give time to prayer and merit, and avoid starting work.
> 
> **🕯️ Lighting the hearth and the first meal**
> 
> At 18:18 on 14 April the woman of the house lights a fresh fire, boils milk in a new pot until it overflows, and cooks milk rice. The overflow is the sign of plenty for the year.
> 
> **💼 First work and the first exchange**
> 
> At 20:18 that evening children open their books, adults do a token piece of their usual work, and younger people offer betel leaves to elders for blessings and a first coin. The family then eats the milk rice together.
> 
> **🌿 Oil and the first trip out**
> 
> Oil is applied to the head at 07:04 on 17 April, followed by a bath facing the set direction. The first trip out to work is at 06:15 on 21 April. A tree is planted at 11:38 on 22 April, a custom that has entered the official list in recent years.
> 
> **🧭 Looking ahead**
> 
> If you want to fit the day around these hours, start with the neutral time on 14 April, since nothing else is begun during it.
